# Results so far (Oct 22, 2026)

Every number below is regenerated by the scripts named next to it. The PUM side is model-only (the integrator, VIPER's closed form generalized), with technology numbers from `lib/tech` (several marked VERIFY). The GPU side is measured on this host's RTX 3080.

## Workload (T, `figs/fig_trace_profile.py`)

- 89 SWE-bench Verified sessions: mini-swe-agent 1.17.5 with mini-coder-4b (Q8_0) on the 3080, a repo-stratified sample. 82 submitted a patch.
- Time on the 3080 splits into **decode 90%, prefill 4%, tools 6%**. Tool time is net of the ~0.28 s container exec overhead per call; raw times are in `results/traces/swebench_summary.tsv`.
- Final context: median 15K tokens, max 64K. **8% of sessions (7/89) ran out of VRAM with fp16 KV** (wall at 32–36K) and had to be re-run with int8 KV at 64K. One of them (xarray-4094, 218 turns) outgrew even 64K.
- Cross-check (G3): replaying each trace through the llama-bench decode curve predicts the measured decode time within −2 to −3%.

## GPU wall (G1, `figs/fig_gpu_wall.py`)

| Weights | KV fp16 | KV int8 | KV int4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q8_0 | 32–36K | 64–80K | 96–128K |
| Q4_K_M | 40–48K | 80–96K | 128–160K |

Q8_0 decode: 7.5 ms/token at an empty context, 14 ms at 32K (fp16 KV). Int8 KV is slower at the same depth.

## Headline design (`experiments/search_compare.py --tag main`)

The Domeline walk finds it in 28 evaluations (45 search sessions):

- Crossbars (ISAAC geometry, transposable K writes) for all weight MVMs, the LM head and attention; 64 fixed-function nonlinear units for softmax, norms, RoPE and SiLU; embedding and sampling on the CPU; CXL-class link; int8 KV. 2.16M arrays.
- **9.42× faster than the RTX 3080 on the search sessions, 9.61× on the 44 held-out sessions.** The Amdahl bound (tools only) is 16.7×.
- Decode is 0.40 ms/token at 4K context and 0.77 ms/token at 64K (3080: 7.5–28 ms). The crossbar read cycle (348 µs/token) is the floor. At long context, score traffic between the crossbars and the nonlinear unit is the next term (transfer: 28% of LLM time).
- Area: **9,729 mm² at ISAAC's 32 nm tile density, ~15× the 3080's die.** Effort: 7 months.

## The walk (`figs/fig_walk.py main`)

0.04× (crossbar MVMs, everything else on the host) → attention to crossbars 0.45× → softmax to nonlinear unit 0.80× → LM head to crossbars 1.39× → norms off the host 1.75× → unit sizing 2.5–6.5× → transposed K writes 7.4× → norms to nonlinear unit 8.5× → 9.42×. Then shrink: 18.8K → 9.7K mm² at the same speed. Every move cites the reading that justified it.

## Search efficiency (`figs/fig_search.py`)

Budget 100 evaluations on the search set; "best" = the smallest design within 1% of the fastest found.

| | Unconstrained | Effort ≤ 5 months |
|---|---|---|
| Domeline walk | 9.42× at eval 28 | 8.00× at eval 33 |
| TPE (3 seeds) | 9.46–9.48× at 77–99 | 3.27 / 7.02 / 8.05× |
| Hill climbing, same moves, time only (3 seeds) | 9.47 / 9.47 / 3.26× | 3.08 / 3.26 / 8.01× |
| Random (3 seeds) | 3.1–8.2× | 0.84–3.06× |
| Sonnet agent + readings | 9.47× at eval 14 | 8.00–8.01× at 11–13 |
| Sonnet agent, time only | 9.47× at eval 6 | 8.00–8.05× at 12–15 |
| Haiku agent + readings (3 seeds) | — | 8.05 / 8.05 / 7.02× |
| Haiku agent, time only (3 seeds) | — | 8.05 / 8.05 / 8.00× |

What this supports:
1. The readings make algorithmic search reliable: the walk always finds the best design in about 30 evaluations; hill climbing over the same single-knob moves, without readings, finds it on 1–2 of 3 seeds.
2. Classical searchers need 3× more evaluations (TPE) or fail (random).
3. **LLM agents do not need the readings in this space.** Their hardware priors, plus the knob descriptions, find the optimum in 6–15 evaluations. Don't claim the readings help LLM agents.
4. At equal speed the walk's design costs less effort (7 vs 10–16 months), because the shrink phase drops the on-die link that only buys the last 0.5%.

## Effort staircase (`experiments/effort_sweep.py`, `figs/fig_effort.py`)

0 months 0.59× (crossbars only) → 1 month 3.07× (vector unit) → 4 months 8.00× (nonlinear unit) → 7 months 9.42× (transposed K writes). TPE finds nothing feasible below 4 months in 150 evaluations.

## Sensitivity (smoke workload; full-workload run pending)

Only the crossbar read cycle moves speed noticeably (0.5×: +10%, 2×: −15%). Per-array area moves area linearly. Write latency, unit throughput and bandwidth, link, and host attention each move speed by ≤3%.
